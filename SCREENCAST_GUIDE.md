# Screencast recording guide

Record Unreal and your code editor with OBS. Use your own narration and save as `SteppingStoneThree_Wilson.mp4`. This outline is preparation material, not a completed screencast.

1. **Project and repository (30–45 seconds).** Show the repository source page, then its commits page including the Module Three implementation commit. Explain that the existing first-person character and resource-harvesting Blueprints were retained. Do not show credentials or private account settings.
2. **Class declarations (45 seconds).** Open `ShelterSystem.h`. Explain `AShelterPiece`, `AShelterSystem`, and `UShelterWidget`. Show the UPROPERTY and UFUNCTION declarations and the inline comments. Explain that ready-to-place supplies are tracked separately from raw materials.
3. **Geometry and recipes (45 seconds).** In `ShelterSystem.cpp`, show `Dimensions`, the mesh constructor, `Configure`, the recipe cost functions, `Resource`, `ChangeResource`, `CanCraft`, and `Craft`. Explain how the system accesses the original Blueprint's Wood and Stone properties, prevents negative inventory, and adds one crafted item only after paying both costs.
4. **Selection and placement (60 seconds).** Show `SelectPiece`, `UpdatePreview`, `Place`, and `CancelPlacement`. Explain the camera visibility trace, ignored player and preview, grid snapping, rotation, ceiling height, overlap box, green/red material, simple collision, and the exact point where a crafted item is consumed. Cancellation retains the item.
5. **Input and widget (60 seconds).** Show `BeginPlay`, `ToggleMenu`, `Tick`, `EndPlay`, widget `RebuildWidget`, `NativeTick`, and the six button handlers. Explain input mode, cursor visibility, movement/look locking while the menu is open, recipe availability, and inventory text. Briefly explain the isolated `RunVerification` helper and show the report.
6. **Live demonstration (2–3 minutes).** Open `smalltestmap` and Play. Press F to show empty inventory and disabled craft buttons. Close the menu. Harvest wood and stone, reopen it, and show the changed counts. Craft each item and show exact deductions and supply increases. Place a floor, walls, and a ceiling. Rotate one wall with R. Show a red blocked preview, move it to valid space, then place it. Cancel another preview and show that its supply is retained. Walk into a placed wall to demonstrate collision. Finish by showing the completed shelter and toggling the widget closed and open.

Before recording, rehearse construction once and gather enough materials for the entire demonstration. If keyboard or mouse controls behave unexpectedly, fix and retest before submission. Do not describe unperformed checks as passed.
